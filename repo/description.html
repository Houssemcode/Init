Init is a fast, calm home screen for LG webOS TVs. It replaces the busy LG home with a clock, the weather, your apps and TV inputs in one row, and a plain, picture or slideshow background.

  • Clock, date and weather: six fonts, any position, and a choice of colours and gradients with adjustable opacity, so it stays readable on light pictures.
  • Fast and smooth: starts almost instantly and scrolls smoothly; the app list and weather refresh quietly in the background.
  • Your apps and inputs: choose what shows, re-order, hide, uninstall, and refresh the list when you install something new.
  • Wallpaper: plain black, one picture, or a slideshow of a folder on a USB drive.
  • Start the TV into Init: add it as a TV input, make the Home button open it, and optionally turn off the LG logo at start-up.

Rooted TV (Homebrew Channel): everything above. Not rooted: the home screen, clock, weather and launching apps work; options that need root are greyed out with the reason.

Tested on LG firmware 03.01.16 (webOS 8.0.0) and newer, webOS 6.x and webOS 4.x. Built with Claude (Anthropic) and tested on real TVs. Screenshots and full instructions are on the project page.
